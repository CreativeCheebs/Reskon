# Change log

What changed in each version of Reskon, newest first: one line per change, in plain words. Versions before 0.6.6 are in the git history (commits called "Version x.y.z: ...").

## 0.7.5 (20 Nov 2026)

- Changed: the phone's "What's next?" sheet now looks like the web box: a label column, drop-downs for the epic and Comes back, Skip at the top, the main button at the bottom right.
- Fixed: the web's Comes back list said "In tomorrow"; it now reads Tomorrow, 2 days, 1 week…

## 0.7.4 (20 Nov 2026)

- Changed: Continue and Next round in the "What's next?" box now look alike: the same label column, and the main button at the bottom right.

## 0.7.3 (20 Nov 2026)

- Fixed: the "What's next?" box lines up: labels in one column, "This one becomes #1" on its own line, and the Start button at the bottom right.

## 0.7.2 (20 Nov 2026)

- Changed: the "What's next?" box now sits at the top of the task panel, under the title, instead of in the middle of the details.

## 0.7.1 (20 Nov 2026)

- Changed: the "What's next?" box in the task panel is about half as tall: the title and its Create button share a line, the epic is one drop-down, and what to copy over is one row of buttons.
- Changed: Next round is one sentence of what it makes, then when it comes back beside the Start button.
- Changed: "Nothing" is now "Skip" in the web box.

## 0.7.0 (20 Nov 2026)

- New: Continue a task: when you finish a task, a "What's next?" box makes the next task that comes after it.
- New: Continue can put both tasks in an epic ("Make an epic for both" is chosen already when the task has none).
- New: the task panel shows a Chain: what a task comes after, and what comes next.
- New: Next round: do the same task again, numbered #2, #3… (or v2, v3 for versions), shown after the title.
- New: the next round can come back right away or snoozed (for example in 1 week).
- New: the task panel lists earlier rounds with their done date, hours and points.
- New: Continue and Start next round are also on the card's right-click menu, and a note after dragging a card into Done offers them.
- New: the phone app has all of this too.
- Fixed: syncing a task into an epic made in the same sync no longer loses the epic on the other device.

## 0.6.6 (20 Nov 2026)

- New: board cards can show priority and level as symbols only (Card settings → "Symbols only").
- Fixed: Sessions day totals, tags and subtypes no longer touch each other.
- Fixed: filter bars no longer start a wrapped line with a stray divider or indent.
- Fixed: quick capture shows one focus ring that fits the box, with room above the hint.
- Fixed: hidden-status lists (Cancelled, Backlog…) keep a readable Title column and scroll sideways when narrow.
